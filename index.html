Privacy Policy

Application: Sit PG Management

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sit PG Management ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized users use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the app may process the following information:

  • Tenant names and phone numbers.
  • Guardian names, phone numbers, and relationships to tenants.
  • Tenant photographs and identity documents, including Aadhaar images, when submitted.
  • Room details, bed allocation, addresses, and joining dates.
  • Rent payment records and PG-related charges.
  • Electricity or other meter readings, where applicable.
  • Login credentials and account information managed through authentication services.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used for legitimate PG management purposes, including:

  • Registering tenants and managing their accounts.
  • Allocating rooms and beds.
  • Maintaining tenant details and documents.
  • Recording rent payments and charges.
  • Managing meter readings and related records.
  • Securing accounts and troubleshooting application issues.
  • Communicating about PG administration when necessary.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

The app uses Google Firebase services for functionality such as authentication, database storage, and file storage. These services may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage, depending on the features enabled.

Service providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Learn more about Firebase privacy: Firebase Privacy and Security .

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessed by authorized PG administrators and personnel for PG operations. It may also be processed by service providers supporting the app or disclosed when required by law.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are intended to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary for PG management, operational purposes, and applicable legal requirements. Requests to access, correct, or delete personal information can be made using the contact details below. Requests may require identity verification and may be subject to legal or recordkeeping obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for PG administration and authorized users. Information about a minor should only be submitted where legally permitted and by an appropriately authorized person. Please contact us if you believe information has been collected improperly.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information associated with the app or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app or its data practices change. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
